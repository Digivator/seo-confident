# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

SEO Confident follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) as its code of conduct. Please read it. It applies to everyone taking part in this project, including issues, pull requests, discussions and any other space where the project is represented.

In short, we want this to be a welcoming and respectful place. Be kind, assume good intent, give and accept feedback graciously, and keep discussion focused on the work. Harassment, personal attacks and discriminatory language are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. They may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that do not follow them, and may restrict anyone whose behaviour they consider inappropriate.

## Reporting

To report behaviour that breaks this code of conduct, email [info@digivator.co.uk](mailto:info@digivator.co.uk). All reports are handled promptly and treated in confidence.
